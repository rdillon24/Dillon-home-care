About Dillon Home Care

Care built around people, not a checklist

Dillon Home Care is part of the Dillon Care Group family, delivering compassionate, non-medical care at home across Gilbert, Chandler, Mesa, Queen Creek, Tempe, and the greater Phoenix East Valley.

Every plan starts with a conversation. From companionship to help with everyday tasks, our caregivers are chosen for compassion first, then matched to fit your household.

Who We Help

Supporting older adults and the families who love them

Dillon Home Care supports older adults who want to stay safely and comfortably at home, and the families who want dependable, non-medical help making that possible — whether that means a few hours of companionship each week or more consistent daily support.

  • Seniors who want to remain independent at home
  • Adult children coordinating care for a parent from near or far
  • Family caregivers who need reliable, occasional relief

About Us

Our Story

Care Should Feel Personal. Because It Is.

Dillon Home Care was created from a simple belief: when someone you love needs help at home, they deserve more than a service. They deserve to be cared for with the same compassion, dignity, and respect we would want for our own family.

Our Approach

Care Built Around People

Founded by Rodney Dillon, Dillon Home Care was built with a vision of bringing a more personal, dependable, and compassionate approach to non-medical home care.

Choosing care for a parent, spouse, or loved one can be one of the most important decisions a family makes. It can also be overwhelming — there are questions about trust, reliability, companionship, independence, and whether the person coming into your loved one's home will truly care.

We understand that the decision is about much more than finding someone to help. It's about finding someone you can feel comfortable welcoming into your family's life.

That is why Dillon Home Care is built around three principles: Compassion. Dignity. Trust. Because good care isn't just about what you do. It's about how you make someone feel.

What Sets Dillon Home Care Apart

A More Personal Approach to Home Care

Every family has a different situation, and every older adult has a different routine, personality, and level of independence. That's why we believe home care should never feel one-size-fits-all.

Care That Respects Independence

Needing assistance doesn't mean someone should lose their independence. We support the routines, relationships, and activities that make a person's life meaningful.

Support for the Entire Family

Home care affects more than the person receiving it. We're here to help adult children and family caregivers feel less alone in coordinating and worrying.

Dependable Support at Home

Families need to know they can count on the people they invite into their loved one's home — professionalism, reliability, and respect are fundamental here.
